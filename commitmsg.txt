glm-radio: "hcxpcapngtool + cat 移出關鍵路徑——轉檔不需要卡". Correct, and it
was being violated three times inside the harvest window. Applied all the way
through: the cold round now runs every operation that needs a living radio
back to back (2.4G harvest, 5G harvest, TXRX read, both rcascan verdicts),
then everything that needs only the guest CPU (both conversions, both cats).
Radio work ends at 54s of a 95s budget instead of the card idling through
30s of conversion mid-flight.

Its D recommendation to merge the harvest and the verdict into one
"--rcascan=active ... -w file" invocation is rejected: hcxdumptool.c refuses
that pairing and calls exit(EXIT_FAILURE) in both option orders, proven
against the installed binary in the previous commit. It checked the help
text and the option semantics in master and 6.3.4, which are all correct, and
missed the two guards at 6041-6047 and 6233-6245.

Bigger gap found while wiring this up: _save_pcap only ever fetched scan_*,
so the cold and sweep rounds left their raw pcapng inside the guest. Only
hashes came back. A hash cannot be turned back into a capture, and the guest
filesystem is erased by a QEMU restart -- and had already filled the disk
once before, which is what made 5G writes return zero packets. The user's
standing instruction is that the captures must survive off the Mac; nothing
in the cold path was honouring it.

New _pull_guest_caps fetches every capture the guest holds after the round,
writes it to capstore, and removes the guest copy on a verified pull. That
also keeps /newroot/tmp off the ENOSPC cliff and stops the backup loop from
re-sending the same bytes each round.

Self-inflicted bug caught by the compiler during the reorder: a string
literal followed by the name CH with no + between them. Implicit
concatenation only joins two literals, so the block would not parse. This is
the exact fragility audit-sweep listed as "correct but fragile" last round.
