fix cold round: --rcascan and -w cannot coexist, hcxdumptool exits

hcxdumptool.c refuses --rcascan together with -w and calls exit(EXIT_FAILURE)
in both option orders. The cold round paired them on its two active phases,
so both died on the spot having captured nothing, and the reporting below
would have logged INJECTION_DEAD -- blaming the radio for the tool's own
argument check. That false verdict could have sent us chasing a hardware
fault on the one insertion we are allowed.

Proven against the installed binary, not just the source: both orders print
"combination of --rcascan and -w is not allowed" and exit 1, while the two
replacement phases fail only on "failed to get interface index" (no card on
the bus), which means every option in them parses.

Harvest and verdict are now separate runs, harvest first on both bands:
a PMKID already proves transmit works, so the verdict only has to tell a
dead radio apart from an empty room. Verdicts use --rcascan=active --rds=5
(APs of all channels sorted by PROBERESPONSE). Reporting reads the tool's
own exit summary, where the PROBERESPONSE line is printed only when its
count is above zero, so a missing line is the verdict; BEACON present with
PROBERESPONSE absent is exactly a card that receives but cannot transmit.

Also corrected two claims in earlier notes: the 5.0s cadence in the guest
log is the printk ratelimit window (DEFAULT_RATELIMIT_INTERVAL=5*HZ, burst
10; 475s/5s=95 windows, (95+1)x10=960 lines, matching the log exactly),
not a driver retry period -- real retry rate is about 18/s. And -71 is a
transaction error, not a Stall, so CLEAR_HALT would not have helped; the
TX status read is an EP0 control vendor request, so the failure is a device
that answers bulk-in but not vendor reads.

Tests: probe_cold rewritten to the new contract, 29/29 including a guard
that no invocation pairs the two options. test_sweepgen ALL PASS on the
Mac. test_round ALL PASS.

Field effect still zero: the card has not been on the bus all session.
